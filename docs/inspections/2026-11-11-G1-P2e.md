## 2. Tokens, primitives, guidelines

- **`src/styles/tokens.scss`:** new block « Clinical record visual language ».
  - Text and surfaces: `--oc-ink`, `--oc-ink-secondary`, `--oc-hairline`, `--oc-tile`.
  - Elevation and shape: `--oc-shadow-soft`; radii `--oc-radius-card` (20), `-tile` (16), `-icon` (12), `-tag` (8).
  - Weight and tabs: `--oc-font-weight-heavy` (800), `--oc-tab-underline`.
  - Colours: one tint/text pair per kind of information, `--oc-info-{blood,allergy,history,care,current}(-tint, -icon)`.
  - The existing tokens are unchanged, so other screens don't move.
- **Contrast** (text on its tint, computed): blood 4.83:1 · allergy 8.69:1 (icon 4.99:1) · antécédents 6.33:1 · care 8.46:1 (icon 5.39:1) · current 9.82:1 · secondary text on a tile 7.33:1. All are at least 4.5:1.
- **New shared primitives** (`src/app/shared/ui`):
  - `oc-tag`: a wrapper around `nz-tag`, with tones `value` (white, hairline), `neutral`, `care`, `allergy`, `history`, `blood` and `current`.
  - `oc-date-block`: day 26 / 800 plus « NOV. », in Tunis time; the full date is read by screen readers; sizes `default` (64×70) and `small`.
  - `oc-file-card`: the whole card is one native button, with a format tile, title, subtitle and action icon. Its accessible name is « Télécharger : Ordonnance, 2 médicaments ».
  - `oc-tabs variant="prominent"`: 3px `#1FBF9A` underline, active label 15 / 700, count badge in the primary tint.
- **Segmented controls** (global, `ng-zorro-theme.less`): #F4F5F7 track, white selected item with a soft shadow, weight 700. The focus ring shows on keyboard focus only.
- **Guard:** `npm run check:ui-boundaries` now fails on `text-transform: uppercase` or a left border of 3px or more in `patient-chart/`, `consultation/` and the new primitives.
- **`docs/ui-guidelines.md` §8:** the seven rules, the token table with the measured contrasts, and the primitives.

## 5. Points reported (not covered by the rules or the mockup, or needing data)

1. **Safety strip loading.** The strip needs the medical summary whatever the tab, so the record view now requests the summary when it opens. Before, it was requested when Résumé was shown, and Résumé is the default tab.
   - What changes: on the chart page opened directly on `?tab=history` or `?tab=documents`, the summary is read once more than before.
   - What stays the same: the endpoint, the audit, the access gate (`clinical`), and RESTRICTED never reads it.
   - **Please confirm.**
2. **Strip with a blood group but no allergy:** the amber strip shows « Groupe : O+ » alone, with no warning icon (the icon belongs to the allergies).
3. **Missing texts** that the mockup doesn't show:
   - a history card without a motif reads « Motif non renseigné »;
   - in RESTRICTED (no motif sent), the card title is « Consultation »;
   - « Dernière consultation » without a diagnosis reads « Diagnostic non renseigné ».

   In RESTRICTED it shows « Consultation » + the doctor, and its button reads « Voir dans l'historique », because nothing opens there.
4. **Diagnostic tag colours:** « Confirmé » uses the primary tint; « Provisoire » and « Écarté » are neutral. The mockup names the tags but not their colours.
5. **Generated-document subtitles and colours outside the three named types:**
   - the documents carry no date of their own, so the certificate subtitle is the consultation date;
   - « Demande d'analyses / d'imagerie » (LAB_ORDER / IMAGING_ORDER, which MRS can produce) use the primary tint and the consultation date.
6. **Documents tab = preview, not download.** The doctor view has no download for uploaded documents: the existing action is « Aperçu ». So the file card shows an eye icon and opens the preview; I added no download (that would be new behaviour).
   - Format tile: PDF / JPG / PNG from the content type, « DOC » when it's unknown.
   - Every file card is primary, since no colour per uploaded type is given.
7. **Mockup date « jeu. 13 nov. 2026 »:** 13 November 2026 is a Friday. The label uses the real weekday (« ven. 13 nov. 2026 »).
8. **« jusqu'au 16 nov. »:** the year is added when it's not the current one (« jusqu'au 2 déc. 2025 »). Long-term drugs show no date.
9. **Historique badge = the server total.** It includes the current and interrupted consultations, and matches the count line.
10. **Global scope of two changes:**
    - The segmented style also applies to the calendar's segmented control.
    - The new text colours (`--oc-ink` #16202A, `--oc-ink-secondary` #4A5562) are used only in the record and the consultation page. Accueil keeps `--oc-text-strong` #17231F; the two are almost identical.
11. **Not restyled:** the expanded « Activité du dossier » timeline (the old administrative history component) keeps its previous look. It is outside the mockup.
12. **No browser render was done in this session**, so pixel-level fidelity to the mockup is unverified.
